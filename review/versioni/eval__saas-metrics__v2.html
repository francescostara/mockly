How to use this mockup

This is not a picture: it is a working report on synthetic data. Everything below works.

Filters

Period. The period selector sits at the top: year › quarter › month. Years available: 2024, 2025.

Segment. Segment — 4 items, list. Pick one and the whole page updates; pick it again to clear.

The "Reset filters" button puts everything back to the initial state.

Clicking inside the charts

In "MRR bridge by segment" click una colonna to filter Segment across the rest of the page.

In "Performance by segment" click una riga to filter Segment across the rest of the page.

Drilling down

In "Performance by segment" every row opens the detail page for that Segment: click the row (or right-click) and come back with the button at the top left.

What does NOT follow the period filters, and why

"Net new MRR by month" always shows the twelve months of the selected year, even when a quarter or a month is picked: the selection is highlighted instead of shortening the line, because a one-month line is a dot.

"New MRR and CAC payback" always shows the twelve months of the selected year, even when a quarter or a month is picked: the selection is highlighted instead of shortening the line, because a one-month line is a dot.

What is not clickable

"narrative": it updates with the filters and shows values on hover, but you do not click inside to filter.
