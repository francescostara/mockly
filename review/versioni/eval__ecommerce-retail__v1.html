How to use this mockup

This is not a picture: it is a working report on synthetic data. Everything below works.

Filters

Period. The period selector sits at the top: year › quarter › month. Years available: 2024, 2025.

Channel. Channel — 4 items, list. Pick one and the whole page updates; pick it again to clear.

Category. Category — 4 items, list. Pick one and the whole page updates; pick it again to clear.

The "Reset filters" button puts everything back to the initial state.

Clicking inside the charts

In "Channel mix" click una fetta to filter Channel across the rest of the page.

In "Repeat rate vs net sales" click un punto to filter Channel across the rest of the page.

In "Acquisition spend by channel" click una barra to filter Channel across the rest of the page.

In "Performance by category" click una riga to filter Category across the rest of the page.

In "Channels for this category" click una barra to filter Channel across the rest of the page.

Drilling down

In "Performance by category" every row opens the detail page for that Category: click the row (or right-click) and come back with the button at the top left.

What does NOT follow the period filters, and why

"Net sales by month" always shows the twelve months of the selected year, even when a quarter or a month is picked: the selection is highlighted instead of shortening the line, because a one-month line is a dot.
